## What if the man she shouldn't want is the only one who pleases her?

- 🕰️ What if he’s older, wiser, and utterly captivated?

Age Gap romance is all about the magnetic pull between lovers at different life stages. Whether it’s a powerful CEO and a fresh-faced intern, or a seasoned protector and a reckless dreamer, these stories explore the tension of experience versus innocence.
